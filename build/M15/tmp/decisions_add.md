306. **M15: the caption data model.** The plan's `captions` array keeps its name and its place (schema 09-plan, the pass schema
    of 09b-text is generated from it), but an entry is now one of the two modes of E3: `mode: word` (a run of 1 to 4 spoken
    words shown one at a time, each on its own spoken time) or `mode: group` (2 to 6 words laid out together). Group entries
    also carry `anchor` (the index of the anchor word), `layout` (kinetic.py's placement of every word: font, size, box in
    canvas px, the group's box, the zone, the motion) and `motion` (a rise of 20 to 40 px or a parallax turn of 3 to 6 degrees
    over the group's life; word mode: a rise of 8 to 16 px or a turn of 2 to 4 degrees over each word's life), and every entry
    carries `importance` (script.json's, which picked the mode). An entry without a mode (a plan written before M15: the
    frozen run and good.json of the M10 to M13 tests) is read as word mode and keeps its old chunk checks (the width of the
    chunk against the 620 px line), so no earlier fixture had to change for the schema. A word-mode entry no longer has to fit
    the caption line as a whole (one word is on screen at a time): the validator checks each word's width at its own size
    (the emphasis word at 1.4 times, in the serif italic) against the line. The text pass no longer splits chunks wider than
    the line. Entry times: each entry runs until the next one starts; across a pause of more than 1.5 s it ends 600 ms after
    its last word; a group never stays into the time a text arrives (the CTA's end card, a chip): its end is clamped to that
    text's start but never before its last word's end. The component hosts: golden.build_project mounts caption-word for the
    word-mode entries (host id `captions`) and caption-group for the group entries (host id `captions-groups`), both with
    `data-track-kind="captions"`, each over its own range; the host ids differ from the component ids, which review/common.py
    read_hosts and the harness already allow.
307. **M15: caption-line is retired, not deleted.** The catalog lists caption-word as vocabulary item 7 (caption-line's place)
    and caption-group as an extra; caption-line.html moved to motion-lib/retired/ (git mv: its history stays) so no catalog
    entry, no component file in compositions/ and no beat-map entry (tokens.py BEAT_TYPES "any") names it. Two earlier-milestone
    things had to follow because the frozen test run was built with it: test-m11 asserts the rebuilt project holds
    compositions/caption-word.html (it asserted caption-line.html), and the frozen run's 10-build/project/index.html and
    instances.json were regenerated by the compiler (only the captions host changed; materialize-project.py keeps the frozen
    index.html over a rebuilt one, so a frozen host naming a file the library no longer has would have failed S12's mount).
    The frozen 09a plan, answer.json, 09c merged.json and 10-build/plan.json set the terminal card's `font_px: 28`, a size the
    plan chose and E4 now rejects (decision 308): the line was removed from those four files (the card draws its default 30
    px; the frozen draft.mp4 keeps the old pixels). test-m9's golden check accepts word runs of 1 to 4 and groups of 2 to 6.
308. **M15: E4's minimum text size warns for what the library bakes in and fails for what the plan chooses.** E4 says no
    graphic text is smaller than the caption size (67 px), but every library component draws its text at the style's own
    sizes (a card title 44, a chip 28 or 30, a terminal line 30, the headline's kicker 21; common.py BAKED_TEXT_PX lists them),
    so a hard rule would fail every reel the director can build and burn its five review rounds on a text size nothing
    upstream can change (the same trap as decision 303's floor, in the other direction). So: a size the plan chooses (the
    terminal card's and the strike-through's `font_px` prop) under 67 px is an error of the validator's `text_size` rule
    (the planted 40 px label); a size baked into a component is one warning that lists them (validator) or the report's
    `warnings` list (S12's mechanical.json); config `visual.text_size_baked` and `review.text_size_baked` set to `error` turn
    the warnings into errors once the library is resized (HUMAN_TODO). A graphic that needs smaller text needs less text:
    the 9b prompt says so beside the 100-character budget.
309. **M15: how E4's 100 characters are counted.** common.py text_items reads every text a component draws (the title, text,
    kicker, keyword, body, line, label and unit props, the lines of a card, window or terminal, a list's items, a table's
    values, a chart's and a grid's labels, the honesty chip's word); the stat number's digits are a graphic and do not count
    (its unit and sub-label do); the DM card's app and time labels do not; the characters are `len(text.strip())` with the
    emphasis asterisks removed. They count from the instance's start to its end (the validator, between every two changes)
    and from the rendered text leaves at every check time (S12, `n` is the full length of the leaf: the old `text` field is cut
    at 60). The CTA scene is left out (the end card's verb, keyword and lines and the DM card are the ask, which the style
    fixes: 200 characters there by design). The golden plan peaks at 95 characters (the terminal card's scene).
310. **M15: the layout engine's contract.** kinetic.py works in line boxes (line-height 1: a word at N px is N px tall), the way
    caption-group.html draws it, so "quick" and "brown" at half the anchor's size are exactly as tall as the anchor ("their
    combined height equal within 2 px" holds to the pixel; a cap-height reading would differ by the fonts' different cap
    ratios, 0.72 to 0.78). The group caption zone is the hero zone between the side margins (x 60 to 1020, y 230 to 1176,
    tokens.json motion.grammar.captions.group_zone); a block fills at most 90 percent of its width, and the block leans to one
    side (left on even groups, right on odd ones: the air on the other side is at least 8 percent of the zone's width, or its
    rows start 24 px apart). The anchor goes from 3.0 times the caption size down to 2.0 until a layout fits; two words are
    stacked beside it at half its size when the group has two or more words after the anchor; the words before the anchor sit on
    a row above it; the rest on rows below, offset to the other side. A group that cannot fit at 2.0 times falls back to a
    smaller anchor (flagged `relaxed`, the validator then accepts under 2.0) only when splitting does not help (layout_groups
    splits first: a word that is itself wider than the zone at 2.0 times cannot be helped). The right-rail rule is applied per
    word: a word whose box reaches below y 960 ends left of x 850; the block moves up or left until that holds. `avoid` boxes
    (graphics on screen with the group) are kept 24 px away, above or below. Fonts: the anchor in the heavy grotesk, the other
    words cycle through the style's serif italic, handwritten (Caveat) and kicker (Space Grotesk) fonts, so three words show
    three fonts.
311. **M15: S7's importance and 9b's choice rule.** script.json's beats carry `importance` (hook, key-claim, number, payoff,
    pre-ask, normal; schema enum). The model may propose one (model.schema.json, the prompt), the code decides what the
    structure decides (script_checks.derive_importance): the hook beat is hook, a number beat number, a payoff beat payoff, the
    beat before the ask pre-ask, a proof beat key-claim, the ask beat normal, and the model's word only lifts a normal beat to
    key-claim, number or payoff. A script from before M15 has none: 9b derives it with the same function (plan9
    importance_by_scene), so the frozen run's number beat (b6) and payoff (b10) are important. The choice rule (finalize_captions):
    without a hint from the director, an entry is word mode when any other text is on screen during it; else group mode when its
    beat is important or its hero zone shows no graphic (E3's "above all when the beat has no clear graphic"), else word mode
    (graphics first). The pipeline re-cuts the words of consecutive auto-group entries of a beat into groups of 2 to 6
    (group_runs: at sentence ends, then near-equal parts at the best break), lays each out (kinetic.layout_groups with the
    graphics on screen as `avoid`), and falls back to word mode with a note when no layout fits beside the graphics. A director's
    own group (`mode: group` and an `anchor` word in the answer) is kept as written, refused with the exact reason when text is on
    screen then or no layout fits. Consequence worth knowing: the real reels' 9a plans put a card on almost every scene, so
    groups appear only where the director leaves a beat without text; M16's prompts for 9a ask for it (HUMAN_TODO).
312. **M15: what S12 adds, and three fixes the new checks needed.** mechanical.py: a group word is judged in the group caption
    zone, not the caption band (the 90 percent size rule stays, a group word never goes under the caption size); at every check
    time the non-caption characters are summed (`text_budget`), a group on screen with other text is `caption_mode`, and every
    caption word and group is read at two times inside its life (35 and 70 percent of a word's, 40 and 80 percent of a group's)
    and must have moved at least review.caption_min_move_px (0.25): `caption_static`, tagged [text] like the other caption
    rules. Check times were added for every group (900 ms in). Three things the golden plan's first mechanical run found, all
    fixed in the shared code: an emphasis term of several words ("same grey shadow") is timed by its first word (the plan's hit
    ms is that word's), an emphasis on a word of a caption group is found as that word's event (no accent box there: the anchor
    and the fonts carry it), and the review looks for a word-mode accent box at `min(150 ms, half the word's life)` after its start
    (a word stays on screen only until the next one starts; a 150 ms word is still half faded in at 150 ms). caption-word fades
    in over at most half the word's life and out over a quarter (never a half-visible word). The golden plan was changed for
    this milestone: its number beat (b6) and payoff beat (b10) lost their cards and chips (a badge stays) so that the voice's
    important lines run as caption groups, as the acceptance test wants ("groups on the important beats"); its other beats are
    as before, with word mode. test-m14 section 4 renders the golden again from this plan.
