# FlashFX brand kit

Source: FlashFX Brand ID 2026 (official brand guide, 48 pages). Every PNG in this folder was extracted from it
with a transparent background. If Gabriele adds original exports from his Business/branding folder with the
same file names, those replace these.

This kit places the FlashFX brand inside reels. It does NOT define the reels' visual style: that is
styles/paper-cards. Brand colors and fonts are documented here so logo placement and FlashFX product
depictions are correct, not to restyle the reels.

## The brand in one line

FlashFX is speed, clarity and creative electricity: high-energy citrus tones, grounded by deep night UI
neutrals, lifted by clean silks and a sky accent. Bold creation (Citrus Splash), momentum (Flush Orange),
focus (Kon), trust and precision (Âbi Blue).

## Name

Always written FlashFX: capital F, lowercase "lash", capital F and X. Never Flashfx, FLASHFX, Flash FX or
Flash-FX.

## Logo system (Logo 2.0)

- Brandmark: the lightning bolt. The primary symbol for app icons, favicons, social avatars and small
  placements where the wordmark would not be readable.
- Wordmark: "FlashFX" set in Inter Bold. For headers, documents and anywhere the name must be readable.
- Combined mark (lockup): bolt plus wordmark. For hero placements, partnerships, product moments, thumbnails.
- Default: the lockup when space allows, the brandmark when it doesn't.

## Logo rules (from the guide)

- Default: the gradient version on black or dark backgrounds. On dark: gradient or white. On light: gradient
  or black.
- If the logo is not instantly readable at a glance, switch to another approved version.
- Clear space: at least 1x the lightning bolt's inner cut width on all sides. In reels, keep at least a quarter
  of the bolt's height of empty space around the mark or lockup, which covers this rule.
- Minimum size in the guide: 24px height for UI icons (32px safer). In reels at 1080x1920: the bolt at least
  64px tall, a lockup at least 300px wide.
- Never: stretch or squash, add outlines, shadows or glows, add any gradient other than the signature one,
  recolor outside the approved files, separate the bolt from the wordmark in a lockup, change the spacing
  inside a lockup, or place the logo on busy imagery or low-contrast backgrounds.
- Never redraw or retype the logo: always place these PNG files. No 3D version of the logo exists.

## Colors

Primary palette:

| name | hex | role |
|---|---|---|
| Citrus Splash | #FFC501 | brand yellow: the primary spark, highlights, key moments, "Flash" energy |
| Flush Orange | #FE6E08 | brand orange: secondary energy, calls to action, emphasis, motion cues |
| Kon | #182235 | foundation: depth, contrast, premium dark UI base |
| Dawn's Early Light | #C9CDD3 | readable neutral: body text, soft UI surfaces |
| Bleached Silk | #F2F2F2 | brand white: clean space, backgrounds, cards, breathing room |
| Âbi Blue | #60A6FA | precision accent: links, active states, info, focus |

Signature gradient (logo only): #FFC501 to #FE6E08, linear, 135 degrees.

Usage:
- Kon as the base for dark UI and high-contrast layouts.
- Citrus Splash is the spark, never a background (no overfilling).
- Flush Orange for emphasis and calls to action, never competing with Citrus Splash in the same moment.
- Âbi Blue for interactive accents (links, active states, selection).
- Black and neutrals (Dawn's Early Light, Bleached Silk) are the majority of the canvas.
- Rule of thumb: 70% neutrals, 20% Kon depth, 10% accent energy. One accent color per layout.
- Do not: multiple accents fighting (yellow, orange and blue at once), neon saturation on large surfaces,
  low-contrast text on textured backgrounds.

Accessibility:
- High-contrast text on backgrounds; readable at a glance on mobile.
- No Citrus or Orange text on light neutrals (use Kon or white text).
- When in doubt: Kon background with Bleached Silk text is the safest pairing.

Secondary palette (from the FlashFX app interface). Only for product UI, mockups and content that depicts the

## Typography (brand materials)

- Inter, signature font. Wordmark and titles: Bold, letter spacing -0.022em (-22 in Canva units), line height
  1.3. Body text: Light, letter spacing 0, line height 1.5.
- Montserrat, accent font. Subtitles and heading 2: SemiBold, letter spacing 0.2em (200), line height 1.3,
  usually all caps. Heading 3: Regular, letter spacing 0.2em, line height 1.3.
- Approved combos: wordmark with a Montserrat heading 3 line under it; wordmark with heading 2 and body text.
- In reels the style's own fonts stay. The brand fonts only appear inside the logo files and inside FlashFX
  product depictions.
